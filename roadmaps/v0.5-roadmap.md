# 0.5 Roadmap - Ultimates
<!-- generated:title:end -->

Ultimate system - the player now not only chose his weapons, but also an ultimate.
This ability gives a huge power spike, and can significantly change the gameplay and dynamic for its duration.

## Cool shits/Style meter charge

The ultimate is charged up by doing "cool shits". There's multiple "cool shits" to do, that acts either as a charge trigger or modifier.

For example, a headshot kill is a cool shit, but it is even cooler if it done at super high speed, after a wall jump, etc.

## Cool shits stale

Some "cool shits" are affected by a stale system, to avoid spamming the same thing over and over, and abusing the system, reward for a more natural gameplay flow instead.

For example, if doing a kill while jumping is a cool shit, the player will tend to just jump all the time. If it is affected by the stale, he might just jump when he actually wants to jump, and earn credit for it, which is more of the intention.

The cool shit system should encourage the player to integrate cool shits in his flow, but not force him.
