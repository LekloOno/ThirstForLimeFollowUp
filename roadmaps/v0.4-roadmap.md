# 0.4 Roadmap - Consumeable items
<!-- generated:title:end -->

Add items that you can pick up, and later use with an item wheel (or other selection mechanism)

<!-- generated:toc:start -->
<!-- generated:toc:end -->

## Health

Slow to use, slows the player down, it is a punishment, player should try to manage his shield efficiently first. It breaks the tempo, and requires the player to find safe covers.

## Shield

Allows to play more aggressively - two variants :
- Light recharge - starts the shield regenerations instantly, so you don't have to wait for the few seconds before the regen triggers.
- Active recharge - starts the regen, and keep it ongoing for a few seconds even if the player takes hit. Allows for a few seconds of super aggressive fight.

## Grenades

Frag, to damage packed enemies, and to rocket jump.

Diverse utilitaries, like a paralyzing explosive, disabling one, explosives that deletes one specific layer of health, etc.

## Quad/Slow mo

Could make the quad and slow mo such items, so you can pick them up and use later.
