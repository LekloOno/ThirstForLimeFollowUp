The project has evolved through a pretty long period of time, developped here and there, so old bits of the code base are riddled with bad design choices, the project overall suffers from a significant technical debts on its older systems.

## 0.5 bis

- The weapon system (composition and definitions of weapon, as well as orchestration of them)
- The movement system (rework the base code, fully isolate the controller from other responsabilities) and notably -
  - Add a step climber, and integrate it flawlessly in the general character's physics.
  - Fix and control tightly slopes interractions.
  - Extract wall interractions as a dedicated and cohesive system.
- The hit registration system (better identify actors and responsibilities, effects, ..)
- Enemies architecture (better isolate and compose behaviors, make them more editable, ..)
