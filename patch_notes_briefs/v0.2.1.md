## Reworks

### Recoil system

Recoil is now fully frame independant !

The system was already "delta-scaled" but accumulation of accuracy errors could lead to significant difference by frame rate.

The recoil system is now fully controlled in the fixed-physics process, and interpollated in frame process to keep the visuals smooth.

### Ledgeclimb
Ledge climb is reworked in various ways -
- The head detection is less strict, allowing easier ledge climb, without having to look clearly in the direction of the edge in case other direction are facing walls
- Detection margins have been adjusted for a smoother experience.
- Out velocity normalized

### Enemies rotation & aim

Enemies do not shoot if you are in their back anymore.

Besides, the enemies have a delay to turn back at you if you're behind them, allowing to fully exploit the backstab mechanic.

They also, in general, never lock their rotation towards you, but instead smoothly lerp their rotation, with various speed.


## Fixes

### Sprint mode setting
Sprint mode would not update properly dued to ambiguous UserSetting.Instance reference.

### Dash cancel
Melee was cancelled by dash if the active weapon was the melee, although it should just sleep it, it is now fixed.

### SwitchTo guard
The direct switch to weapon was bugged if you holstered the same weapon previously.

## Optimizations

### Further occlusion culling
The map is now fully covered in occluders and occluded entities.

### Enemies
Enemies have been slightly optimized to keep decreasing their CPU cost, by spreading and delaying the AI workload, and disabling some functionnalities when possible.

### Ui buttons sounds
A few ui buttons sounds suffered from the previous menu overhaul, and missed their hover/click sounds.

Besides, score board entries now also have a hover/click sound.
