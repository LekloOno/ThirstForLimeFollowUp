# v-0.2.3 - minor update

## News

### Fists charge UI

There's now a UI element to show the current charge level of the fists, as well as if it is ready to shoot or not.

### Armor/Barrier sounds

Hitting an enemy with armor or barrier now produces a specific hit sound.

## Changes

### Hit sounds rework

Slightly reworked the sound design of hits and kill.

Besides, the kill sounds gets its pitch scaled with killstreaks.
