# 0.3 - A full scale experience to confront the core mechanics
<!-- generated:title:end -->

0.2 was a very early combat-focused sandbox.

It was mostly restricted to combat and a bit of combat movements.

Thus, the movement kit wasn't really shining much, a good movement player wouldn't do much difference.

In general :
- Pathing choice and strategy was very limited.  
- Routes, rollouts, and complex transition movements were very weak.  
- Angle play and general positionning wasn't so important and the player wasn't really incentived to manage his shield into an attack-defense cycle, he could easily remain engaged for the whole duration.
- The game was especially repetitive, with very low variety of combat environment.

## 0.3 Goals

This updates aims at scratching the surface of the limitations cited above.

To do so, we are bringing the core game mechanics developped previously in a full scale map and mode.

Such mode remains extremely simplified, but exposes the player to rotation routes, transitions, and thus also a more jerky game tempo. Not straight 80 seconds of pure fight, but intense fights interspered with recovering and rotation phases.

It will notably allow to exploit the movement kit much further, and explore the scale that matches the desired flow of the game.

## Approach

### Full scale map

A new, full scale map - divided in sectors and subsectors, that explore different geometries and scales for different types of engagement.

- Garage sector, long range and open.
- House sector, short range, clustered and very technicall with a bit of verticallity.
- Chemical sector, diverse, with medium, short and long range sections with clearer structure.
- Pit, the old pit remains, pure intense close range fight

There's multiple intricated routes to connect each sectors for the player to discover and exploit.

### Sector mode

To exploit this map, a new game mode comes with it.

The map is divided in sectors. They are picked (pseudo) randomly. Once a sector is activated, a UI element will show its location to the player, and bots will spawn near this sector.

Once the player has killed all the enemies, a new random sector will be activated.

The player has 3 minutes to score as much as possible by eliminating bots.

**Sector hierarchy**

A little detail about sectors - the picking isn't fully random. Sectors are divided in sectors, sub-sectors, sub-sub-sectors, and so on, in a tree like structure.

This ensures the player doesn't have to travel over the entire map over and over. He will sometimes have long rotations, but most will happen inside one major sector, thus with minimal transitions.
