## Brief - Tackling v0.1 feedback

v0.2 mostly aims at finding long term solution to the first feedbacks of v0.1.

Besides, the version also came with many fixes, reworks, but an important point was also optimization, so that the project could run stable on very limited hardware.

The benchmark was an old gen i7 with no dedicated gpu, with the goal of stable 60 fps.

## 0.2 Goals (sandpit)

Many things have been reworked, changed, or added, but the most important ones were notably reloading and general movements accessibility.

Reloading was one of the biggest feedback issues. It felt frustrating and unintuitive.

Many things have been put to make it feel more natural, notably a completely new and dedicated reloading system, with intricated mechanics, like partial cancels, separated reloading steps like unloading, loading, chambering, etc., but also new UIs and sounds. 

Some movement abilities didn't feel so intuitive, or could benefit from automation processes, typically an auto sprint that required a very refined tuning and design.

Many more have been worked on, you can find the details in the different patche notes for [v0.2.x](v0.2.x/).
