# v-0.1 - Sand pit arena

v0.1 is the first playable iteration of the game.

## 0.1 Goals

It is 80 seconds of pure fights in a small and clustered arena, with very basic approach of base concepts the games wants to tackle.

That is movement (combat movements as well as rotation movements), aiming, and micro-decisions.

These are tackled in extremely minimal aspects in this version.

**Combat movements** are leveraged by making the bots accuracy depends on the speed of the player relative to their vision (moving straight to a bot doesn't affect its accuracy, but straffing does).

**Rotation movements** are leveraged with the quad damage and slow-mo pickups that appear 30 seconds after the beginning of the game, and can be accessed in various ways, using different movement techs, for different strategies, costs, efficiencies, risks, etc.

**Aiming** is leveraged by, eh, having pure fight for 80 seconds straight, with bots that can have pretty heratic movements in hard and normal modes. Besides, barrier bots incentivize the use of tracking weapons, thus tracking technique, where armor bots incentivize the use of clicking weapons, thus dynamic clicking technique.

**Micro-decision** is leveraged by always setting up the player against multiple enemies at different angles. Positionning becomes quite important to break angles and avoid cross fire.

The game also includes a working online leaderboard for players to compete !
