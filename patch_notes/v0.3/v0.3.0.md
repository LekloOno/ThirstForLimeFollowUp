## News

### Sand pit evolves !

The map evolves into a full scale map - divided in sectors and subsectors, that explore different geometries and scales for different types of engagement.

- Garage sector, long range and verticality.
- House sector, short range, clustered and very technicall with a bit of verticallity.
- Chemical sector, diverse, with medium, short and long range sections with clearer structure.
- Pit, the old pit remains, pure intense close range fight

There's multiple intricated routes to connect each sectors for the player to discover and exploit.

### Sector Mode

The game mode is adapted to this map update.

#### Brief

The map is divided in sectors. They are picked (pseudo) randomly. Once a sector is activated, a UI element will show its location to the player, and bots will spawn near this sector.

Once the player has killed all the enemies, a new random sector will be activated.

The player has 5 minutes to score as much as possible by eliminating bots.

#### Details

A little detail about sectors - the picking isn't fully random. Sectors are divided in sectors, sub-sectors, sub-sub-sectors, and so on, in a tree like structure.

This ensures the player doesn't have to travel over the entire map over and over. He will sometimes have long rotations, but most will happen inside one major sector, thus with minimal transitions.

## Changes

### Hit sounds

Hit sounds have been reworked, re-mixed.

### Health balancing

Player's health has been re-balanced.

Managing shield properly is now crucial, as it is a little longer to start regenerating, and flesh health is much lower.

This incentivizes the player to create a true aggressive/defensive rythm.

### Ledge vault direction

The ledge vault can now be directed with the player's camera and movement inputs. It is not constrainted to the normal of the climbed ledge anymore. This provides a new tool to redirect momentum.

Besides, the ledge vault now has a much higher minimum boost, allowing the player to be launched at a significant speed even if he was not moving before performing it.

### Snappier vacuum

The player's item vacuum is now a little stronger and snappier.

## Fixes

### Reload cancel

This one wasn't a bug, just a straight oversight in the code. If the player canceled reloading before he unloaded his weapon, reload would restart automatically. This is the intended behavior if he unloaded the weapon, but if he did not yet, the player should be able to truly cancel the reload.

### Amo item floor clipping

Ammunition would sometimes not be pushed by the floor, and stop floating. They would just lie on the ground.

Now fixed.

### Camera bobbing

Camera bobbing animation code was a bit buggy, and amplitude would notably scale with framerate, making bobbing extremely strong with high frame rates.

The code has been entirely reworked to be better controlled, and fully frame-rate independant.

### Weapon system

The weapon system is some old crappy code. It's riddled with bugs waiting to explode :DD

It is the next refactoring target, but for now, we're putting tape on the shit pile. Here are some ?? fixes.

#### Loadout init bug

If the run ended as the player was not on the initial weapon (g0z-brt), that weapon won't be correctly initialized the next run, and he won't be able to use it until he swaps weapons, which re-enables it.

#### Melee buffer
