## Reworks

### Reload overhaul

A new reload system, with explicit steps, more flexible cancels, automation and more visual and audio cues.

#### Reload system steps

The system is split in different successive mechanical steps, that correspond to "real-life" reloading actions.

Some are optionnal depending on the type of weapon (not all weapons require a separated unloading phase)

Some can be skipped under specific circonstances (no chambering required if there's already a bullet in chamber)

- **Unload**
  - **optionnal** depending on the weapon's type.
  - **step** in which currently loaded ammos are unloaded, to let new ammos be loaded (for example, removing the emptied magazine)
- **Insert**
  - **step** in which news ammos are inserted in the weapon, for example, a magazine or a shell. (for now, it is thought as a bulk insert, but it can later be adapted to support successive individual shells/bullets loading)
- **Chamber**
  - **optionnal** depending on the weapon's type.
  - **step** in which an ammunition is loaded in chamber.
  - **skipped** if a bullet was already in chamber, for example if the player did not fully empty the magazine on an automatic weapon.
- **Recover**
  - **step** to get the weapon operationnal after completing the full reload.
  - **skipped** if the player cancels the reload during this step, for example by switching weapon, quick melee, etc.

#### Cancel flexibility & depth

When reloading is cancelled, restarting the reload will only start at the last reload step (except for recover step which is thus completely skipped), making cancel less punitive, and reloading more flexible and fluid.

For example, if you cancel reload as the magazine is already inserted, but you haven't chambered a bullet yet, when you come back to this weapon and reload, you will only have to chamber and recover, not go through the whole reload process again.

The system introduce a little more depth too, as you can intentionnaly ensure to always keep a bullet in chamber on chambered weapons, to skip this reloading step and optimize your time, or cancel the recover for some other actions, etc.

#### Reload system automation

Reload will automatically start if the player shoots dry, that is, a shot is triggered as he has no ammunitions left.

Reload will also automatically start if the player starts reloading, passes the unloading step, cancels the reload, then comes back to the weapon, as the weapon cannot operate anyway if it is unloaded.

This helps making the experience more fluid and intuitive.

#### Visual & Audio cues

New UI elements are introduced for different scenarios -
- Low ammunitions (loaded)
- Low ammunitions (total)
- Very low ammunitions (total)
- No ammunition left.

New sounds are also introduced -
- A sound starts playing when the loaded ammunitions starts getting low, and rises, more and more audible, as the loaded ammunitions gets close to empty.
- A subtle sound plays when the player try shooting dry.
- Modular sounds plays to follow the different reloading steps and transitions.


### Movement reworks and QoF

#### Ledge climb

Ledge climb do not preserve momentum anymore, except while performing a superglide vault.

#### Sprint

##### Cancel

Slide, Crouch and jump do not cancel the sprint anymore, making it a little more fluid to use, and require less user inputs.

##### Modes

It is now possible to chose between different sprint modes
- Toggle (press to start/stop)
- Hold (sprint as long as pressed down)
- Auto (a specific automation process)

##### Auto sprint

Here also comes the new auto sprint mode
It tries to trigger a sprint whenever it is possible, but stops on shooting, and restart at the end of a shooting grace period that can be configured in the user's settings.

The player can also quick start the auto sprint to override the grace period, with a dedicated configurable bind.

### Menus & Settings overhaul

The settings menu have been completely reworked.

#### QoF

The visuals and interractions of settings are enhanced :
- Clearer visuals, more compact and readable
- Explicit categories
- Confirmation dialogs on abort/apply

Other menus benefit from :
- Confirmation dialogs where needed (quit, leave, surrender)
- Some explicit return button

#### More options

A new "video quality" category under video section.
- Quality preset to quickly control all quality settings
- Shadows
- Ambient occlusion
- Screen space indirect lighting
- Glow
- Screen space reflection
- Anti aliasing (not yet very stable with current outlines shader)

## Changes

### Movement 

#### Sprint cancel

Don't cancel sprint on slide/crouch/jump, just delay.

#### LedgeClimb

Do not keep momentum, except if player is supergliding.

### Armor buff
Armor was too weak (and probably still is). Headshot with the G0Z-BRT were clearly enough, so it got buffed.

### Pickups and pickups Spawners
Pickup spawners can now be one shot (spawn the item only once), or have a different "first spawn" time, initial respawn time, than their normal cycle respawn time.  
Besides, pickups and vacuum are now snappier, for a more predictable and less frustrating experience.

## Fixes

### Death crouched bug fix

Crouch wasn't properly reseted on exit tree as it didn't have the time to physic-check the uncrouching.

### Sequence spawner

The last remaining bits of the old system have been cleaned. The sequence spawner is finally truly free of spawning bugs ! You should not see enemy teleport or disappear anymore.

## Optimization

### Projectile visual trails

Projectile visual trails have been optimize to drastically reduce the CPU load.

### Shadows

The shadows pipeline has been reworked to allow for smooth static shadows, even on low-end devices.
