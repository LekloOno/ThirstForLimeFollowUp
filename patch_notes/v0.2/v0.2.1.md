## News

### Low health cues
When the player's normal health is low, a red vignette starts to pulse, more and more visible as the player gets close to death.

Besides, when the player's total health is low, the sound effects gets muffled, and a slight reverb plays.

### Flat background ambient sound
A simple Background wind sound that plays to give a little more life to the scene.

### ScoreBoard entries sound
Score board entries now also have a hover/click sound.

## Reworks

### Recoil system

Recoil is now fully frame independant !

The system was already "delta-scaled" but accumulation of accuracy errors could lead to significant difference by frame rate.

The recoil system is now fully controlled in the fixed-physics process, and interpollated in frame process to keep the visuals smooth.

### Ledgeclimb
Ledge climb is reworked in various ways -
- The head detection is less strict, allowing easier ledge climb, without having to look clearly in the direction of the edge in case other direction are facing walls
- Detection margins have been adjusted for a smoother experience.
- Out velocity normalized

### Enemies rotation & aim

Enemies do not shoot if you are in their back anymore.

Besides, the enemies have a delay to turn back at you if you're behind them, allowing to fully exploit the backstab mechanic.

They also, in general, never lock their rotation towards you, but instead smoothly try to follow you.

### Enemies spawning

Now, bot can spawn in a more flexible way, randomly along some predefined spawn points instead of a random position in a given radius. This lets them spawn in more diverse places.

## Changes

### Backstab angle
Backstab is now a little more forgiving, with a slightly wider angle.

### Double jump
Slightly balanced the double jump. It is now less expensive, and physics is slightly adjusted.

## Fixes

### Sprint mode setting
Sprint mode would not update properly dued to ambiguous UserSetting.Instance reference.

### Dash cancel
Melee was cancelled by dash if the active weapon was the melee, although it should just sleep it, it is now fixed.

### SwitchTo guard
The direct switch to weapon was bugged if you holstered the same weapon previously.

### Ui buttons sounds
A few ui buttons sounds suffered from the previous menu overhaul, and missed their hover/click sounds.

### Keybind savings
With the new dialog system in settings, a little bug got in, and it was impossible to save persistently new bindings.

### Melee damage buff
Melee would not benefit damage, although it should.

### Enemies aim/rotation AI
Fully separated the aim and rotation AI of enemies, to avoid possible conflicts that were happening, leading to the enemy completly stopping to shoot.

### Enemies spawning inside walls
No check was done for enemies possibly spawning inside walls, as I considered it rare enough to bother for now, but according to some feedback, it can happen more often than expected.

This spawn method has kinda be ditched anyways, a new method is explaiend in the reworks section !

## Optimizations

### Further occlusion culling
The map is now fully covered in occluders and occluded entities.

### Enemies
Enemies have been slightly optimized to keep decreasing their CPU cost, by spreading and delaying the AI workload, and disabling some functionnalities when possible.
